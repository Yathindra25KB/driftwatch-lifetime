# Code of conduct

DriftWatch is a small research project. Everyone who opens an issue, comments, sends a pull request or asks a question is expected to be respectful, patient and constructive.

## What we expect

- Disagree with the evidence, the method or the wording, not with the person. A critique of a result is welcome and is the point of a pre-registered study.
- Say what you read or ran, and give the file or claim id, so a reader can check it.
- Respect people's time and privacy. Do not post other people's personal data.
- Keep to the scope in `docs/ETHICS.md`: this project describes how detectors decay; it does not accept malware samples, evasion techniques or adversarial-example generators.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, discrimination, sustained disruption of a discussion, and sharing malware, credentials or private information.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a problem by email to the maintainer at the address in `CITATION.cff`, or use GitHub's report function. Security vulnerabilities go through `SECURITY.md` instead. The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block accounts that break these rules. Reports are handled in confidence.
