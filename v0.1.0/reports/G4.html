Gate G4 evidence (Phases 8 and 9: Tier 2)¶

Gate: G4, end of week 19 (Sun 21 Feb 2027).

  • Question (PHASES.md): are E3 and E4 complete or honestly reported, do prereg-v2 and prereg-v3 predate their results, and is this file written?

  • Stop rule: if Tier 2 is incomplete, the rest becomes future work and there is no Tier 3.

Prepared: 21 Oct 2026, by Claude Code (engineering lead). Sources: docs/CLAIMS.md (E-40 to E-58, P-17, P-18), reports/tables/e3_*.csv and e4_*.csv, and the reviews in docs/reviews/.

The gate decision is the researcher’s (CLAUDE.md). This file states which criteria the evidence meets and what is open.

Decision (researcher)¶

G4: PASS, decided by the researcher on 21 Oct 2026 after reading this file.

The researcher also reported:

  • Phase 9 hours: “10 to 18 h”, given as a range.

  • Cumulative hours: within plan.

  • Recent hours: at least 9 h per week over the last four weeks.

  • Capstone deadline: earlier than Sun 28 Mar 2027 (end of week 24).

The Tier 3 deadline rule therefore fails. Under the Phase 10 prompt, Phase 10 runs in polish mode (MODE B) with no discussion. The decision is recorded in reports/G5-tier3-decision.md.

Summary¶

All three G4 criteria are met on the evidence.

  • E3 and E4 are complete: E3 has 56 of 56 cells and E4 has 76 of 76, with none failed.

  • Registration order: both registrations predate their first results.

  • This file exists.

Tier 2 was run in calendar week 2 of the plan, against a plan of weeks 15 to 19, so no stop rule is near.

E4’s result is negative for the monitors. That is a reported result, not an incomplete one.

  • No monitor beat periodic retraining in any stratum.

  • In nine rows a monitor was slightly worse.

  • When refits happened made no difference, to within the 0.01 margin.

  • The monitors alarmed in most slots.

What was cut¶

Nothing registered for Tier 2 was cut. The registered reductions, stated in the addenda, are these:

  • E3 ran a reduced grid: two delays, two budgets, and the ablation and score cells at 5 seeds.

  • E4 ran one budget (1%), two delays, and one alarm level per monitor (alpha 0.01, frozen, never tuned). It has no label-based detectors and no monitor-triggered labelling.

These are design limits, not cuts made after results.

Reviews¶

  • Leakage, E4 code (docs/reviews/leakage-review-e4.md): no blocker and no major finding. Six minor findings and four notes are fixed or recorded, including:

    • a label-invariance test;

    • a complete cache key;

    • the matched control’s settings check;

    • refusal of evidence runs without the tag.

  • Statistics, E4 (docs/reviews/stats-review-e4.md): no blocker; six major findings, all on interpretation (saturated monitors and M-C, repeated BODMAS arms, reversals inside the margin, BODMAS drops in one window, replicate handling, missing chance baselines), all resolved; CLAIMS E-59 to E-65 supersede or extend E-50 to E-57.

  • Synthetic acceptance of the monitors (code behaviour, never evidence): reports/tables/e4_synthetic_checks.csv.

    • Every monitor alarms in the shift slot.

    • The no-drift false-alarm rates were stated before the tag (ADR-0010).

    • Two monitor designs were fixed before any real-data run.
