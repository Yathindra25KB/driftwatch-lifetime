## How to read the table

Three axes of this study, as in the guide:

- **Delay**: does the work treat the time a requested label takes to arrive as a parameter?
- **Budget**: does it fix or study the number of labels an analyst can buy?
- **Conformal or monitor**: does it use conformal p-values, or a drift monitor, as a signal?

"Not in the abstract" is not "no".
